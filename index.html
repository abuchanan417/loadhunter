Math and rules

FMCSA hours of service on every round: 11-hr driving and 14-hr window after 10 off, 30-min break after 8 hrs driving, a rolling 70 hrs in 8 days (the oldest day's hours come back at midnight, and the planner waits for that recap when it beats a 34) or a 34-hr restart. Waiting at a shipper burns the 14 unless it runs 10 hrs. The clock only resets when the trailer drops in Springfield for the next driver; passing through on the way to the delivery keeps the same driver and his clock. Planning caps driving at your daily number, never the full 11.

Add miles or fuel from an export
Paste or open an ELD IFTA state-mileage report (needs State and Miles columns, plus Date and Unit) or a WEX transaction export (needs State and Gallons, plus Date, Vehicle, Amount, Product). Map

Filled marker is the stop the truck is heading to. Color is on time (green), at risk inside 2 hours (amber), late (red) or no ETA yet (gray), from the driver's ETA, or from his last position with legal driving hours. Stops sit on the exact spot once the driver arrives with GPS, on the street address once it's entered, otherwise on the city.
